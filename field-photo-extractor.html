Field Photo Extractor

Pull full-resolution photos back out of field apps. Notability photos come out at the size they were stored, and also with your handwriting redrawn on top at that same resolution. FieldMove photos are renamed by locality and geotagged.

runs in your browser · nothing uploaded
Drop notes, project folders or exports here

Folders, zips and several sources at once are fine. Everything is read locally.

Notability

Photos, with and without ink

Drop a .note export (Share → Note), or on a Mac the note's .nbn folder from iCloud Drive › Notability. A PDF export also works for the photos alone, but Notability may downsize them.

FieldMove

Photos named by locality

Drop a .fm project folder, or a CSV export folder together with its photos. Each photo gets its locality, time and note in the filename, and its GPS position written into the file.

Gaia GPS

Waypoint photos

Drop a .kmz export to unpack its photos. For .geojson and .gpx, photo links on waypoints are downloaded at full size where Gaia allows it, and listed otherwise.

Apple Freeform boards can't be read from a web page because Freeform keeps them inside its own app storage. The command-line version handles Freeform as well as everything above: field_photo_extract.py (Python 3; python3 field_photo_extract.py --help).

Originals are copied byte for byte. Renders with ink are new JPEGs (PNG when the source photo was a PNG) at the photo's own pixel size.
