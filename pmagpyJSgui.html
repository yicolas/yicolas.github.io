⬡ FITQUEST — how to play

Controls

click slotset the mode (LT / MT / HT) — it stays on until you change it. A single click on a point (step strip or any plot) selects/deselects it and the window is remade contiguous each time: deselect an endpoint → window shrinks by one; deselect an interior point → it stays filled (to really drop it, right-click to skip); click outside the window → it extends and fills the gap. Dropdowns set exact from/to bounds.
dropdownsthe from/to selects in each slot set a contiguous range in one go — then click points to drop or add any
right-clickskip / unskip a point everywhere (the point skipper — excluded from all fits)
copy / pasteat the bottom of the console: pick a scope (all / LT / MT / HT), copy this specimen's fits, then move to another specimen and paste — the windows transfer by matching temperature/field, so they land on the right steps even if the step list differs. The clipboard survives reloads.
auto-skipon load these are skipped automatically: NRM and LN/LN2 steps (set by the drop menu); earlier runs of a repeated treatment (only the last rerun kept); and any measurement with csd ≥ 12° (bad measurement). All are defaults — right-click any step to override, and your choices are saved.
queue sort/filterthe bar atop the specimen queue sorts within each site by MAD, dang, grade, # fits, ChRM-first or name (↑/↓ toggles direction), and filters to unsaved / saved / flagged (MAD>10 or dang>15) / usable-ChRM / fit overlap (a step shared by two components — usually a mistake to fix).
⭳ MagICexports a complete MagIC 3.0 contribution for all loaded sites — locations, sites (Fisher site means), samples (orientations), specimens (each fit in both geographic and tilt-corrected frames with MAD/dang), and measurements (all steps) — in one upload-ready fitquest_Laos_magic.txt. ⭳ .redo likewise writes every fit from every site into one file.
dropwhich pre-demag steps are left out of every fit: the NRM, the first liquid-nitrogen cycle (LN, the first 77 K step) and any later cycle (LN2, or a step labelled LN2). Default drops all three. Changing it re-applies to all loaded specimens, including ones you have saved; other skips (reruns, csd, right-clicks) are untouched. Remembered between sessions.
⤓ autowhen lit, a fresh backup fitquest_<site>_autosave.redo is downloaded to your Downloads shortly after each save (debounced). A local file:// page can't overwrite a file in your repo, so this is a download — your browser may append (1),(2)… Keep the newest; it's always current. Toggle off if the Downloads clutter bothers you. Your fits also persist in the browser regardless.
eq-area menussite picks which site (split by name prefix — NWFA, NWFC, PKSF…; "current" follows the selected specimen), boulder drills into one sub-sample within that site (e.g. NWFC-3), level aggregates at specimen / sample / site / all, component filters LT / MT / HT. Sample & site views add a Fisher mean (diamond) with α95 ring; clicking a star opens that specimen.
⬡ congconglomerate-test overlay: one Fisher mean per boulder across the active site (each a labeled, colored diamond with its specimens faint behind), plus a Watson (1956) randomness verdict on the clast means — R < Ro → RANDOM, passes (pre-depositional); R ≥ Ro → CLUSTERED, fails (likely remagnetized). Uses the ChRM/terminal component unless you set a specific one.

House rules (how you're graded)

Free lines only (DE-BFL) — never anchored to the origin. MAD is per segment against its own points. Only the terminal (last-unblocking) component is judged on origin-trending: dang is shown for the fit that ends on the last surviving step. Usable ChRM: MAD ≤ 10° and dang ≤ 15°. A beautiful MAD on the wrong segment is worth nothing — watch dang. Fit the terminal segment first, then split what precedes it. Don't assume three components; one good fit beats three forced ones.

Scoring

per fit: 100 − 6·MAD − 2·max(0, dang−5) + 2·(n−3) [cap +10]
+15 bonus for a usable terminal ChRM. Grades: S ≥ 95 · A ≥ 85 · B ≥ 70 · C ≥ 50 · D ≥ 30 · F. Streak counts consecutive specimens saved with a usable ChRM. Skipping > 25% of a segment's points caps its grade at C — beyond that the line is wrong, not the points.

Easiest: drag the whole repo folder in

Drag the Laos repo folder (the one containing Magnetometer_Data_corrected) onto the drop zone. The app finds the best .redo inside it — preferring Magnetometer_Data_corrected/demag_gui.redo, the manuscript fits — loads every specimen it names, and queues them. This is one gesture and works even when the page is opened as a local file:// (where the folder-picker button is disabled). When a specimen exists in several places it prefers paths containing corrected and avoids _archive/safety/old copies.

Or drop a .redo on its own

Drop a .redo file and the app asks for your data root once (remembered in Chrome when served over http/https), then resolves its specimens. If it reports 0 found, you pointed it at a folder without the specimen files — drag the repo folder in instead. .sam files beside the matched specimens set the site grouping and queue order. Fit names map lt → LT, lt2 → MT, mt (the ChRM) → HT, and keep their original names and colors on export. Dropping a folder with no redo inside loads all its .sam sites.

Output

⭳ .redo exports every fit in demag_gui format (thermal bounds in K, AF in T) — import it straight into demag_gui. Progress autosaves locally per specimen. Drop an existing .redo with your files to resume previous picks.
