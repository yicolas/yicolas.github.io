Overview

Cheap, reproducible, lightweight pellet press for portable XRF and geochemical analyses. Works by hand or with standard caulk gun. Made for pXRF measurements of fresh clays and fine sands — extrudes and drains water as pellets are pressed.

Usage

Homogenize sediments with mortar/pestle first. Assemble with sediment inside: [1 2 <sediment> 3 4]. Enter into caulk gun and press. Clean thoroughly after use; let pellets dry before analysis.

Applications

Environmental monitoring, sediment provenance, real-time stratigraphic logging, clay/sand mineralogy assessment. Produces consistent XRF tablets with minimal sample preparation.
