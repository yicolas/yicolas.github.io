Overview

Many attempts have been made across multiple groups to develop a portable and reliable in-field pellet press for portable XRF and other geochemical analyses. From car jacks to hydraulic presses, most choices are cumbersome and time consuming. Here we introduce a cheap, reproducible, lightweight and durable pellet press for use by hand or caulk gun.

This model was made specifically for pXRF measurements of fresh clays and fine sands, and will extrude and drain water as the pellets are pressed. Pellets work best after drying.

Usage

Sediments to be used in the press should first be homogenized to a consistent grain size using a mortar and pestle to reduce matrix effects. Pieces are assembled with sediment inside and then entered into the caulk gun as: [1 2 <(sediment inside) 3 4]. After pressing, thoroughly clean equipment and leave pellets to dry.

Why Pellet Pressing?

Pellet pressing is widely adopted for XRF sample preparation because it produces homogeneous, stable, and accurate samples efficiently and cost-effectively. Pulverizing samples into fine powder (<100 µm) and pressing them into dense pellets mitigates matrix effects from grain size and texture variations. The smooth, flat surface ensures consistent X-ray penetration and reduces scatter, improving analytical precision.

Research Applications

The sediment press is especially useful for producing consistent XRF tablets from unconsolidated sediments with minimal sample preparation. This allows rapid geochemical analysis in the field for environmental monitoring, sediment provenance studies, real-time stratigraphic logging during coring or drilling, and quick assessment of clay/sand mineralogy.
