Overview

Collection of geological maps, field observations, and structural analysis from various research campaigns and field courses. This compilation represents work from multiple field areas across the western United States, documenting diverse geological settings and structural styles.

These maps represent field-based geological investigations conducted during research projects, field courses, and independent studies. The collection includes detailed structural maps, stratigraphic columns, cross-sections, and field sketches that document complex geological relationships in areas of significant tectonic activity and geological interest.

Field locations include sites in California, Nevada, Arizona, and other western states, covering a range of geological settings from Precambrian basement exposures to Mesozoic sedimentary sequences. The maps incorporate data from traditional field methods including compass-based structural measurements, stratigraphic logging, and detailed outcrop description.

This work has contributed to understanding regional geological relationships, supported paleomagnetic sampling campaigns, and enhanced interpretation of complex structural and stratigraphic problems. The mapping experience gained through these projects has been fundamental to developing field skills and geological intuition essential for research in structural geology and tectonics.
