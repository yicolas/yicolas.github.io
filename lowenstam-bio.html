About

Biography of Heinz Lowenstam, pioneering geobiologist, written by Joseph Kirschvink. This biographical memoir chronicles the life and scientific contributions of one of the founders of modern geobiology.

Heinz Lowenstam was a pioneering scientist at Caltech who fundamentally transformed our understanding of biomineralization and the role of organisms in geological processes. His work demonstrated that living organisms can precipitate a wide variety of minerals and that these biominerals have distinct characteristics that can be preserved in the geological record.

Among Lowenstam's many groundbreaking discoveries was the identification of magnetite in chiton teeth, which represented the first demonstration of biogenic magnetite production. This discovery opened up entirely new fields of research into biomineralization and magnetoreception in organisms.

Joseph Kirschvink, as a student and colleague of Lowenstam at Caltech, provides unique insights into Lowenstam's scientific approach, his influence on the field of geobiology, and his lasting impact on subsequent generations of researchers. This biography serves as both a historical document and an inspiration for current and future geobiologists.
