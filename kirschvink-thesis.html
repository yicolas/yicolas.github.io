About

Original scans of Joseph Kirschvink's groundbreaking PhD thesis on paleomagnetism and the geomagnetic field. This historical document represents pioneering work in the field of paleomagnetism and has influenced generations of researchers in the field.

Dr. Joseph Kirschvink is a professor of geobiology at Caltech and has made fundamental contributions to our understanding of paleomagnetism, magnetotaxis in bacteria, and the role of magnetic minerals in biological systems. His PhD thesis laid important groundwork for many of these subsequent discoveries.

These original scans preserve an important piece of scientific history and provide insight into the development of paleomagnetic methods and theory during a critical period in the field's evolution. The thesis demonstrates the rigorous analytical approaches and careful experimental design that have characterized Kirschvink's career.

Access to historical scientific documents like this thesis helps current researchers understand the foundations of their field and appreciate the evolution of scientific thinking and methodology over time.
