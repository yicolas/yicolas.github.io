Reading the images

All imagery is Sentinel-2 at 10 m: cloud-screened medians of the June–October dry season. In the false-colour panel (near-infrared, shortwave infrared, red) water is dark purple, vegetation orange, and bare sand or tailings pale grey-green.

The strip along the bottom is the best clue. Rivers move and bars reshape every year, while ponds appear suddenly in a field of tailings and then fill in. Forest regrows slowly over abandoned mining ground.
